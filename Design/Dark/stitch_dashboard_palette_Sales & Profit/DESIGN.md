## Brand & Style

This design system channels an uncompromising, nocturnal aesthetic that fuses dark synthwave atmosphere with modern cyberpunk luxury. Inspired by the moody, late-night sonic and visual world of Chase Atlantic, the interface reflects neon reflections over wet asphalt, high-performance machinery, and twilight architecture.

The target audience encompasses high-agency operators, creative technologists, audio engineers, and luxury fintech consumers who demand precision without sterile corporate blandness. The aesthetic rejects cheap sci-fi clichés in favor of architectural rigour: dark, tinted plum canvas depths, structural borders, luminous neon strikes, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy.

Key style traits:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep plum-midnight tones construct an expansive, low-light backdrop that preserves visual stamina and heightens high-contrast data.
- **Selective Luminescence:** Neon pink and electric blue are restricted to functional signifiers, focus states, and critical data surges rather than blanket decoration.
- **Architectural Edge:** Polished structural cards framed with precise midnight-violet borders.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined nocturnal hierarchy anchored in deep chromatic violets rather than flat grays. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF1CC0`):** Neon Hot Pink. The primary call-to-action engine, crucial trend indicators, active state indicator, and critical warnings.
- **Secondary (`#B131FA`):** Electric Violet. Secondary interactions, gradient fills, and auxiliary status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#1C3AFF`):** Electric Blue. Cybernetic accent, data series contrast, informational cues, and hyperlinked states.
- **Deep Accent (`#4A0074`):** Midnight Violet. Subtle structural badge backings, table header fills, and gradient lowlights.
- **Canvas Base (`#0C0817` / `#130924`):** The midnight root layer providing velvet absorption.
- **Surface Elevation (`#1C1033`):** Dark purple card surface for actionable container blocks.
- **Border Trim (`#2E1B4E`):** Distinct structural divider separating modules cleanly.

### Text Contrast Tiers
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#F8F6FC` (Primary titles, essential metrics).
- **Text Mid-Contrast:** `#B4A8D1` (Body copy, table data, field titles).
- **Text Low-Contrast:** `#6D5C8A` (Inactive values, timestamps, hints).

## Typography

The typographic engine uses a tripartite system built for analytical luxury:
1. **Space Grotesk** powers headlines with geometric tension and sharp alternate cuts, grounding the design in forward-thinking cybernetic craft.
2. **Geist** anchors prose, settings, and complex interface copy with neutral Swiss precision and ultra-refined kerning.
3. **JetBrains Mono** governs metrics, telemetry readouts, badges, uppercase system chips, and data charts.

All labels utilizing `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` and `label-md` should render uppercase with expanded letter-spacing to reinforce technical rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width `1600px`), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile. 

Layout rhythm is governed by a strict 4px base increment. High-density data dashboards pair tightly packed interior card spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`) with generous spatial separation between macro panels (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Outer canvas margins clamp to `margin` (`1rem`). Complex analytic dashboards stack into singular column units.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Gutter locks to `1.25rem` with `2rem` margins. Metrics panels switch to a 2x2 modular matrix.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Expanded margins (`3rem`) and gutters (`1.75rem`), with multi-pane toolbars and sticky telemetry sidebars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional drop shadows in favor of tinted luminescence and luminous ambient boundaries.

### Depth Archetypes
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid backdrop at `#0C0817` overlaid with an ultra-subtle radial gradient blooming `#130924` toward the screen center.
- **Level 1 (Structural Surfaces):** Panel backgrounds at `#1C1033` paired with 1px border strokes in `#2E1B4E`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Elevated Cards):** Surface elevated to `#231440` with top-edge inset highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 28, 192, 0.25)`).
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Modals):** Semi-opaque frosted backings at `rgba(28, 16, 51, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient diffuse glow: `0 16px 40px -10px rgba(74, 0, 116, 0.6), 0 0 2px 1px rgba(255, 28, 192, 0.3)`.

### Neon Halo & Focal Accents
To prevent optical fatigue, neon glows are treated with strict restraint. Primary interactive nodes receive an ultra-tight neon halo on hover/focus: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(255, 28, 192, 0.45)`. Electric Blue active data lines apply a soft glow: `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px rgba(28, 58, 255, 0.6))`.

## Shapes

The system implements a controlled, technical shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Soft 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding provides just enough contour to prevent harsh mechanical corners while maintaining the surgical, engineered stance of high-end automotive cockpits and developer workstations.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Chips):** `0.25rem` (`4px`).
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) (`rounded-lg`).
- **Modals & Flyouts:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Status badges and numeric metric tags alone leverage full-pill radius (`9999px`) to create an immediate shape-distinction against data containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#FF1CC0` fill with `#0C0817` high-contrast bold typography (`JetBrains Mono` or `Space Grotesk`). Hover triggers `0 0 18px rgba(255, 28, 192, 0.5)` and shifts surface brightness.
- **Secondary:** Surface fill of `#1C1033` with a 1px border in `#B131FA` and text in `#F8F6FC`. On hover, border illuminates to `#FF1CC0` with inset violet glow.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text in `#B4A8D1`. Hover illuminates text to `#FF1CC0` with a background shift to `rgba(74, 0, 116, 0.3)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill geometry (`rounded-full`), height `24px`, font `label-sm`. Background `rgba(74, 0, 116, 0.4)` with 1px border in `rgba(177, 49, 250, 0.4)` and a 6px glowing indicator dot matching `#FF1CC0`, `#1C3AFF`, or `#00FFA3` (operational).

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#1C1033` with 1px border in `#2E1B4E`.
- Card headers feature uppercase `label-md` trackers in `#B4A8D1` accompanied by sharp metric displays (`headline-md`) in `#F8F6FC`.
- Interactive cards elevate on hover with a border transition to `#B131FA` and top edge highlight.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Dark plum hollow fill `#130924`, 1px border `#2E1B4E`, text `#F8F6FC`.
- **Placeholder:** `#6D5C8A`.
- **Focus:** Border snaps to `#FF1CC0` with a subtle focused spread `0 0 0 2px rgba(255, 28, 192, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px boxes with `0.25rem` radius or 16px circles. Inactive border `#2E1B4E` with `#130924` interior.
- Checked state fills with `#FF1CC0`, displaying a stark `#0C0817` micro checkmark or center pip, accompanied by a faint pink aura.

### Data Visualization & Telemetry
- Area charts feature glowing stroke lines in `#FF1CC0` and `#1C3AFF` falling into linear vertical gradients that dissolve into `rgba(28, 16, 51, 0)`.
- Gridlines utilize ultra-faint borders in `rgba(46, 27, 78, 0.5)` with monospaced axis callouts in `#6D5C8A`.

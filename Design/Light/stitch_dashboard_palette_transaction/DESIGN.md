## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the quiet prestige of heritage financial editorial design with the surgical precision of modern executive intelligence tools. Drawing inspiration from high-grade analytical journalism and tactile publications (Monocle, Stripe Press, Financial Times, Bloomberg Businessweek), the visual aesthetic discards cold, sterile tech minimalism in favor of an architectural, warm-editorial ethos.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Authoritative & Discerning:** Uncompromising typographical rigor and disciplined information hierarchy deliver immediate executive trust.
- **Tactile & Humanistic:** Warm alabaster and organic paper-like undertones evoke physical stationery, reducing screen fatigue during intensive data review.
- **Architectural & Rigorous:** Hairline borders, structural rhythm, and disciplined tabular data matrices establish order without decorative clutter.

### Target Audience
C-suite executives, institutional portfolio managers, policy architects, and strategic analysts who demand dense, legible data synthesized through an uncompromising, high-taste interface.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on Plus Jakarta Sans, selected for its clean geometric baseline, subtle humanistic contours, and impeccable tabular numerical alignment.

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Contrast:** Headlines leverage tight tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.035em`) to evoke the dense authority of financial broadsheets.
- **Tabular Precision:** All numerical displays, rates, indicators, and delta badges must enforce open-type tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during data shifts.
- **Uppercase Hierarchy:** Meta-labels, category tags, and table column headers utilize `label-caps` styled with `text-transform: uppercase` and expanded tracking (`+0.08em`) to anchor analytical grids.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric, structured 12-column grid system inspired by editorial broadsheets and balance sheets, shifting from expansive macro-gutters to high-density content panels.

### Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max layout width capped at `1440px` for sustained readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex dual tables stack into split panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Side-by-side analytical tools collapse into stacked horizontal sliding sheets.

### Hairline Grids & Structural Dividers
Separation is driven primarily by 1px hairline dividers (`#e8e2d8`) rather than thick container blocks. Vertical dividers align tightly with column gutters to maintain an architectural, editorial rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface stacking and subtle ambient warmth, avoiding cold grey drop-shadows or stark digital skeuomorphism.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level 0 (`#fbf9f5`):** Base canvas background across the entire viewport.
2. **Structural Tier 1 (`#ffffff`):** Standard data cards, analytical modules, and main articles. Defined by a crisp 1px border (`#e8e2d8`) coupled with an ultra-soft, warm ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 36, 52, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(27, 36, 52, 0.02)`.
3. **Elevated Tier 2 (`#ffffff`):** Popovers, dropdown menus, and hover states for primary intelligence cards:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(27, 36, 52, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(27, 36, 52, 0.05)`.
4. **Modal / Overlay Tier 3 (`#ffffff`):** Focused executive dialogs:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`. Paired with a warm translucent backdrop (`rgba(27, 36, 52, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette combines tailored, architectural structural containers with refined pill badges.

### Geometry Rules
- **Structural Cards, Panels, & Tables:** Use soft `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.5rem` (8px) corners to preserve a crisp, paper-like edge.
- **Buttons & Action Controls:** Soft `0.375rem` (6px) roundedness conveys functional clarity.
- **Status Pills, Tickers, & Metadata Chips:** Full roundedness (`9999px` / pill) provides visual counterpoint against rectangular tabular modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Japanese Indigo (`#1d4ed8`), text `#ffffff`, `0.375rem` radius. On hover: `#1e3a8a` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus states display an outer 2px offset ring in `#2563eb`.
- **Secondary (Editorial Wire):** Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e8e2d8`, text `#0f172a`. On hover: border `#ded7ca`, background `#faf7f2`.
- **Destructive:** Border 1px solid `#dc2626`, background transparent, text `#dc2626`. On hover: background `#dc2626` with text `#ffffff`.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Executive Pill Badges:** Height 24px, padding `0 10px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `label-caps` typography.
- **Accent Tag (Terracotta):** Background `rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.25)`, text `#c2410c`.
- **Performance Tag (Jade):** Background `rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.25)`, text `#047857`.
- **Neutral Tag:** Background `#f4efe6`, border 1px solid `#e8e2d8`, text `#1b2434`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Composed with surface pure `#ffffff`, bordered by `#e8e2d8` (1px).
- Internal structural divisions (header to body, body to summary footer) use `#e8e2d8` hairline dividers.
- Section headers display an uppercase tracked category label (`label-caps`) alongside large tabular metrics.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#ffffff` fill, 1px border `#e8e2d8`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`. On focus: border shifts to `#1d4ed8` with a 2px diffuse ring (`rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hairline borders `#ded7ca`, filling with `#1d4ed8` and a crisp white indicator when active.

### Lists & Tabular Data
- Table headers utilize `label-caps` in `#64748b` with an underlying 1px border in `#e8e2d8`.
- Rows feature subtle bottom hairlines (`#f4efe6`), highlighting with background `#faf7f2` on hover.
- Numerical columns strictly align right with `label-numeric` and tabular figures enabled.

### Supplemental Executive Components
- **Key Metric Delta Block:** Large display figures in `#0f172a` paired with positive/negative trend pills (`#047857` / `#dc2626`) and micro sparkline graphics.
- **Lead Editorial Callout:** Inset quote card with a left border accent (2px solid `#c2410c`), warm background `#faf7f2`, and italicized executive summary copy.
